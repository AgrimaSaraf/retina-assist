# RetinaAssist research questions

## RQ1 — Decision support
Does AI assistance improve clinician grading accuracy and/or decision time for retinal fundus cases?

## RQ2 — Explanations
How does showing a model-attention explanation affect appropriate reliance, perceived trust, and decision confidence?

## RQ3 — Automation bias
When the AI is incorrect, does showing the AI before or after an independent clinician judgment change the probability that the clinician adopts the incorrect recommendation?

## RQ4 — Uncertainty
How does model confidence/uncertainty presentation affect appropriate acceptance and rejection of AI advice?
