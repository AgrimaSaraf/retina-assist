# Draft research questions

These are hypotheses, not findings.

## Study A — screening

- How does AI presentation change clinician decisions?
- Does explanation change appropriate vs inappropriate reliance?
- How do model confidence, clinician confidence, and case difficulty interact?

## Study B — follow-up

- What proportion of eligible visits miss recommended follow-up?
- Which routinely available approved variables are associated with missing follow-up?
- Can a simple model beat operational rules and remain calibrated?
- Can a human-centered workflow improve staff usability and, after appropriate approval, follow-up adherence?

The project must accept a null result or the possibility that ML adds no value.
