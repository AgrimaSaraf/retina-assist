# Clinic workflow discovery interview guide

Use this before deciding what AI should do.

1. Walk me through a normal patient visit.
2. Which tasks do you repeat most often?
3. What takes longer than it should?
4. Where are patients most often confused?
5. Where do staff re-enter/search for information?
6. What happens after follow-up is recommended?
7. How do you know whether the patient returns?
8. Which follow-ups require the most manual chasing?
9. Which records are frequently incomplete/inconsistent?
10. Are duplicate/mismatched records a problem?
11. What creates bottlenecks?
12. What information do you wish appeared automatically?
13. What part of retinal-image workflow is hardest?
14. Which automation would you not trust?
15. What mistake would be especially harmful?
16. If you could eliminate one problem tomorrow, what would it be?

Capture: role, pain point, frequency, impact, current workaround, measurement idea, required data, safety/privacy concerns, and whether AI is actually necessary.
