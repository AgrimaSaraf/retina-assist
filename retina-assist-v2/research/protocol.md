# Research protocol scaffold

This document is not ethics approval.

## Phase 1 — discovery
Observe clinic workflow and interview staff. Identify a real recurring measurable problem.

## Phase 2 — retrospective baseline
With appropriate approvals, define eligible visits, adherence window, exclusions, missing-data strategy, and baseline outcome.

## Phase 3 — modeling
Compare prevalence baseline, simple rules, logistic regression, then optional nonlinear models.

## Phase 4 — HCI design
Prototype the minimum workflow intervention. Conduct staff usability sessions.

## Phase 5 — prospective evaluation
Only after appropriate clinic/ethical/privacy/research approvals.

Predefine primary outcome, allocation method, sample-size rationale, consent requirements, control/comparator, analysis, safety monitoring, and stop rules.

Do not call the study an RCT unless it truly is randomized and appropriately governed.
